## Verified / reusable

- The business name displayed is **Airport Express**; the site also uses “Airport Express San Francisco.”
- The site currently publishes **415-775-5121** and **info@airportexpresssf.com** on its homepage and contact page.
- **910 Harrison Street, San Francisco, CA 94109** appears on the live contact page and repeated footer. Confirm whether this is still the correct public mailing/contact address before making it prominent in the new site.
- The current site lists **SFO** and **OAK** airport transportation.
- The current site offers airport rides, hourly/direct rides, and custom trip inquiries. It describes hourly/direct transportation around the greater San Francisco Bay Area and references wine country, one-way rides, multiple stops, weddings, and special requests.
- The current site links to [https://airportexpresssf.com/reservation](https://airportexpresssf.com/reservation), a page titled “Check Rates and Make Reservations.” The page and its fields were visible during review, but no test reservation was submitted; current form operation and payment flow are **not verified**.
- The current homepage and navigation link to Groups and Charters, but that page could not be reviewed. The user brief independently confirms Groups & Charters as a core service.
- The user-provided positioning authorizes language about transportation throughout an **approximately 60-mile service area**. The current site's exact origin, boundaries, and eligible destinations are not established. New copy must not present a precise boundary or guarantee a named city.
- Existing homepage SEO title: “Airport Express — Airport Shuttle in San Francisco.” Existing meta description: “Reliable transportation to or from SFO and OAK. Or book our hourly rate option for chauffeured trips to wine country or anywhere you need to go.” Use the broader user-approved positioning for the redesign while keeping descriptions factual.

## Needs confirmation

- Confirm the current phone, email, and whether 910 Harrison Street is appropriate to publish as a customer-facing address.
- Confirm that the online reservation page is still the preferred booking system, works on mobile, and can receive all four service types. Confirm whether its custom-trip route remains `/reservation?types=inquire`.
- Confirm current airport pickup instructions for SFO and OAK, including which pickup points and terminals apply and when travelers should call.
- Confirm current operating hours and whether any service is available 24 hours.
- Confirm how “approximately 60 miles” is measured, from what origin, whether it describes a radius or general operating vicinity, and which destinations can be accepted. Until then, use only the user-approved approximate wording with a clear invitation to call about a destination.
- Confirm current Groups & Charters details and the correct inquiry path.
- Confirm the “Since 1979” claim before using it as a trust statement. It appears on the current homepage but was not independently corroborated.
- Confirm whether the older Airport Express van photo remains representative of the current fleet and is approved for reuse.
- Confirm whether the existing logo files are the latest official artwork and whether a vector/high-resolution master exists.
- Confirm policy language (cancellation, refunds, ticket exchanges, luggage, pets, child seats, flight delays) before publishing it.

## Outdated / conflicting

- The homepage header says “24 HOUR PICK UPS,” while the policy page states operating hours of 4:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. FAQ pickup windows also vary by SFO/OAK and San Francisco pickup area. Do not publish hours until the owner reconciles these statements.
- The FAQ and policies say child seats are not provided and customers must bring one; the reservation page says child seats can be provided upon request. Do not state availability or requirements until confirmed.
- Older FAQ/policy statements about SFO walk-up pickup, OAK advance reservations, exact airport terminal curb instructions, and airport-to-airport transfers may no longer be operationally correct. Do not use them as current instructions.
- Older pages publish prices, extra luggage fees, child fares, cancellation/refund rules, and ticket terms. These may change and must be reconfirmed before reuse.
- The contact form displayed both a sent-success message and an error message in the page text during crawl; form delivery was not tested.
- The footer still shows a 2023 copyright notice. Do not carry the year forward as evidence that the rest of the page content is current.
- The current navigation includes affiliate registration, employment, coupons/tickets, and policies. These do not need to remain in primary navigation for the redesign.

## Do not use without client approval

- Exact pricing, fees, discounts, fares, refund/cancellation terms, or ticket rules.
- Exact vehicle types, fleet age/availability, passenger or luggage capacities, or luxury/service-level claims.
- Child-seat, pet, mobility/accessibility, or other accommodation claims.
- Operating hours, 24-hour claims, response-time promises, flight monitoring, or availability guarantees.
- Exact coverage boundaries or named destination guarantees beyond the airports and broad Bay Area context noted above.
- “95%” last-minute reservation success, “100% satisfied,” prestigious hotel relationships, “premier” status, or other comparative/reputation claims from older pages.
- Any airport pickup directions from the legacy site until verified.
